Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers. Learn more about our process, capabilities, and deliverables.

What technology stack does Gb-Technologies specialize in?

We specialize in full-stack web development using modern technologies including Python, Django, JavaScript (React, modern ES6+), HTML5/CSS3, and Bootstrap, deployed on secure cloud environments.

How long does a typical web development project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A modern business website or landing page typically takes 1 to 2 weeks, while full-scale custom web platforms and applications usually take between 4 to 8 weeks. We provide clear milestone roadmaps before development begins.

Do you offer post-launch maintenance and technical support?

Yes, we provide comprehensive post-launch maintenance packages. These include regular security updates, uptime and performance monitoring, continuous backups, and rapid bug resolution to ensure your application runs smoothly around the clock.

Can you redesign or upgrade an existing website or platform?

Absolutely. We can perform a thorough code and UX audit of your existing platform, modernize outdated technology stacks, optimize performance and mobile responsiveness, and migrate your database with zero downtime.

How do we collaborate and communicate during the development cycle?

We practice transparent agile project management with regular progress updates, interactive demos, and dedicated communication channels. You will always have full visibility into your project's trajectory and deliverables.

How do I get started with Gb-Technologies on a new project?

Getting started is simple! Reach out through our contact form or email us directly at gburethomass@gmail.com with your project outline. We will schedule a discovery consultation to discuss your vision and prepare a tailored proposal.
